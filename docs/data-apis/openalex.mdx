## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `OPENALEX_API_KEY` | some calls | Optional free OpenAlex API key (openalex.org account); raises the daily budget 10x and the rate limit from 10 to 30 requests per second. Sent as the api_key query parameter. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Works without a key on a small daily budget; a free key gives &#36;1 of usage a day, resetting at midnight UTC, and is needed to follow cursor paging past the first page.
- Rate limit: 10 requests/second keyless, 30 with a free key; semantic search 1 per second.
- page/per_page paging stops at 10,000 results (page * per_page); use cursor=* for more. per_page max is 100.
- group_by returns up to 200 groups per page and pages only by cursor.
