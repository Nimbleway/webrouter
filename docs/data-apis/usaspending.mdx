## Notes

- Keyless and free; no account.
- Most searches are reads over POST with a JSON body; every path ends in a trailing slash, as documented.
- Award searches require filters.award_type_codes (contract types A, B, C, D; assistance types 02-11; IDV types IDV_A to IDV_D) and take filters such as keywords, time_period [&#123;start_date, end_date&#125;] in YYYY-MM-DD, agencies, recipient_search_text, place_of_performance_locations, naics_codes and psc_codes.
- Amounts are US dollars. Paging is limit plus page, with page_metadata.hasNext in the response.
