## Notes

- Keyless. Queries are throttled per IP: too many and the service refuses further queries for a while. Its terms forbid high-volume automated querying.
- Fields tagged [Non-Public Data] are withheld under privacy law; the terms in each answer say how to request them.
- The service's own help answer (/help) is its only published query list: domain, nameserver, entity, nameservers?ip= and entities?fn=.
