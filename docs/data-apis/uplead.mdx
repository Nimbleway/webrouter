|---|---|---|

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- 500 requests a minute; X-RateLimit-* headers, and Retry-After on a 429. 403 means a paused account or no subscription.
- Every response carries userInfo.availableCredits.
- Saved lists and the reseller-only preview are not in this entry.
