## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `MANGOOLS_API_KEY` | required | a Mangools API token (mangools.com/api-token), sent in the x-access-token header |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Lookups draw from plan quota pools (daily for keywords, SERPs and metrics, monthly for backlink rows); mangools.limits shows what is left. A spent pool answers 429 with type CreditLimit. Identical requests within 24 hours come from cache and cost nothing.
- Burst limit: 3 requests per 30 seconds per account and pool; send requests one at a time, at least 11 seconds apart in the same pool. Over it: 429 type RateLimit with retry_after seconds.
- Locations are Google geotarget ids (2840 is the United States); find them with mangools.locations.search.
