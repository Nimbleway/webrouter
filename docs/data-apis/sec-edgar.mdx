## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `SEC_EDGAR_USER_AGENT` | optional | the User-Agent the SEC asks automated clients to declare: a company or name and an admin contact email, e.g. 'Sample Company Name AdminContact@example.com' |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Keyless, but the SEC asks every automated client to declare a User-Agent with a name and contact email; set SEC_EDGAR_USER_AGENT.
- Fair access: at most 10 requests per second across all your machines; heavier use is throttled.
- CIKs in data.sec.gov paths are zero-padded to 10 digits (CIK0000320193). Map a ticker to its CIK with tickers.list.
- XBRL data covers standard taxonomies (us-gaap, ifrs-full, dei, srt) only, not company-custom tags. For bulk work the SEC publishes nightly companyfacts.zip and submissions.zip instead.
