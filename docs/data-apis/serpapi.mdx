## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `SERPAPI_API_KEY` | required | a SerpApi private API key (Your Account page at serpapi.com) |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Every engine is the same GET /search with a different engine value; the engine's own parameters follow.
- Billing counts each successful uncached search against your monthly quota; cached results (identical search within 1 hour) and the Account and Locations APIs are free.
- Results come as JSON; search_metadata.status goes Processing then Success or Error, and search_metadata.id is the archive id.
