## Notes

- Keyless and free, licensed CC BY 4.0: credit PRH as the source.
- Only companies registered or pending in the Trade Register are covered.
- A Business ID (Y-tunnus) is 7 digits, a hyphen and a check digit, e.g. 0112038-9.
- Results come 100 per page. A page past the last returns the first page on company search but an empty answer on the financial statement calls. Too many requests answer 429.
