## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `OPENREGISTER_API_KEY` | required | An OpenRegister API key (openregister.de/keys). Sent as a Bearer token. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Credits per call: autocomplete 1; company, owners, holdings, financials, contact, person, searches and insolvency 10; UBO and historical owners 25; realtime=true adds 10. The free plan has 500 credits a month; 402 means out of credits.
- Company ids look like DE-HRB-F1103-267645; get them from a search or autocomplete first.
- Monitors, Transparenzregister credential storage and extract orders, and background jobs are not in this entry.
