## Notes

- Credits: validation 1 per request; enrichment 1 per place for standard attributes plus 1 per place per research field.
- Limits: 10 requests a second and 120 a minute per key; 429 carries Retry-After.
- Existence confidence says a business existed at the address; it does not by itself say it is open today.
- Bulk jobs (up to 10,000 places) are async: submit, poll GET /v1/jobs/&#123;batch_id&#125;, then download the gzipped JSONL from the presigned URL (valid about 15 minutes). At most 20 jobs in progress per key.
- 402 means the balance cannot cover the request's credit hold; nothing ran.
