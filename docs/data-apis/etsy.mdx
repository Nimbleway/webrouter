## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `ETSY_API_KEY` | required | Your Etsy app's keystring (etsy.com/developers/your-apps). |
| `ETSY_SHARED_SECRET` | required | Your Etsy app's shared secret, from the same page. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Every call sends x-api-key as keystring:shared_secret. These public reads need no OAuth token; an app's own shop data, orders and every write are left out.
- Rate limits are per app, shown on the Your Apps page and in x-limit-per-second, x-remaining-this-second, x-limit-per-day and x-remaining-today headers; over the limit answers 429 with retry-after.
- Prices come as &#123;amount, divisor, currency_code&#125;: the price is amount / divisor.
- Paging is limit (max 100) and offset.
