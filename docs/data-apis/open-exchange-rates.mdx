## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `OPEN_EXCHANGE_RATES_APP_ID` | required | An Open Exchange Rates App ID (openexchangerates.org/account/app-ids). Sent as the app_id query parameter. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- The default base is USD; other bases need a paid plan.
- Every call counts against a monthly request quota (usage.json and currencies.json do not); update frequency depends on the plan.
- All dates and timestamps are UTC.
