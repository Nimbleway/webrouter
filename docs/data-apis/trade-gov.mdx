## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `TRADE_GOV_API_KEY` | required | A free ITA subscription key (developer.trade.gov: sign up, subscribe to the Data Services Platform APIs product, copy the primary key from your Profile). Sent in the subscription-key header. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Only active entries are in the API; for historical research use each list's own source.
- A match is a prompt for due diligence, not a determination; check the entry's source list and its notes.
- Paging: size up to 50, offset at most 1000, so narrow with sources, types or countries for broad queries.
