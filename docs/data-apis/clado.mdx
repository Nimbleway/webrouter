## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `CLADO_API_KEY` | required | A Clado API key (starts with lk_). Sent as a Bearer token. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Credits cost &#36;0.01 each; you pay only for successful calls (5xx are not charged). See each endpoint's notes for its price.
- Rate limits are per endpoint and tier, e.g. Search People 20/min free up to 400/min; contact enrichment is unavailable (0/min) on the free tier. A 429 carries retry_after in seconds.
- Bulk contact enrichment and job cancel are documented but not in this entry.
