## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `EVENT_REGISTRY_API_KEY` | required | An Event Registry / NewsAPI.ai key (eventregistry.org settings page). Sent as the apiKey query parameter. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Every call spends tokens from the plan: a search of the last 30 days is cheap, and each older year searched costs more. Suggest lookups are the cheapest way to resolve a name into the URI the filters need.
- Filters take URIs, not names: resolve a concept, category, source, author or place with the event-registry.suggest.* calls first.
- Languages are ISO 639-3 codes (eng, deu, spa...), not two-letter codes. Dates are YYYY-MM-DD.
- The text-analytics calls (annotation, categorisation, sentiment) and the minute streams are not in this entry.
