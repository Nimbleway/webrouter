## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `MENTION_API_KEY` | required | A Mention access token. For your own account, the token shown at the bottom of your API app's settings page works without the OAuth flow. Sent as a Bearer token. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Every call is scoped to an account id: call mention.account.me first to read yours, then mention.alerts.list for the alert ids.
- Dates are ISO 8601 with an offset, e.g. 2024-11-16T19:20:30+01:00.
- Some resources are rate limited, with limits that vary by resource and method; a 429 carries X-Rate-Limit-Reset, a Unix time.
- Alert, tag, share and task changes, and marking mentions read or favorite, are writes and are not in this entry.
