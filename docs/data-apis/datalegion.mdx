## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `DATALEGION_API_KEY` | required | A Data Legion API key (dashboard, after sign-up). Sent in the API-Key header. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Prepaid credits: 1 credit = 1 record returned; the price per credit depends on the product bought (base or premium, with or without contact data). Credits-Used and Credits-Remaining response headers report the charge.
- Rate limits per account: enrichment 100/min, search and discover 10/min (search 5/min on a free trial); 429 carries Retry-After.
- Free-trial person results redact contact data until a card is verified.
- Search takes SQL without LIMIT/OFFSET: use limit, offset (max 10000) and cursor. A 408 timeout is not charged.
- Bulk enrichment and data-issue reports are documented but not in this entry.
