## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `COMPANIES_HOUSE_API_KEY` | required | A Companies House REST API key (developer.company-information.service.gov.uk &gt; Your applications, a live application). Sent as the HTTP Basic username with an empty password. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Rate limit: 600 requests per five minutes.
- Company numbers keep leading zeros (00000006). Paging is items_per_page plus start_index.
- Filing documents themselves are served by the separate Document API, not in this entry.
