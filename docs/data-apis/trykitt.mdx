## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `TRYKITT_API_KEY` | required | a Kitt API key (API section of your Kitt dashboard) |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Real-time mode (realtime true) answers in the response; it is throttled by your concurrent request limit and over it answers with an error to retry.
- HTTP 402 means a rate limit or insufficient funds; the body says which. 401 means a missing or wrong key.
- With realtime false, a job is queued and its result is fetched with trykitt.job.get.
