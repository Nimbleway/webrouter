Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Calls spend credits from your plan; some optional fields add credits only when they return data (listed under champs_supplementaires). Check the balance with pappers.credits.usage.
- Parameter names and values are French. Dates in search filters are JJ-MM-AAAA (day first).
- A SIREN is 9 digits (the company); a SIRET is 14 (one establishment).
- Lists, notifications and webhooks are writes and are not in this entry; the document downloads (PDF/XLSX) are left out too.
