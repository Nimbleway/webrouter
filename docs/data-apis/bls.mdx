## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `BLS_API_KEY` | required | a free BLS Public Data API v2 registration key (register on bls.gov/developers; the key arrives by email). Sent as the registrationkey query parameter. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- v2 limits per key: 500 queries a day, 50 series and 20 years per query, 50 requests per 10 seconds.
- Series ids are uppercase codes and may contain _, - and #; lowercase letters and other characters are rejected. The popular-series and survey endpoints help find them.
- With no years given, a series call returns the past three years. Years are four-digit YYYY values.
- The optional catalog, calculations, annualaverage and aspects outputs need the registration key.
