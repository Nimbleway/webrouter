Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Priced in credits: USD 0.05 per credit pay-as-you-go, 0.04 on a subscription. Every response reports credits_consumed.
- Alternative-data endpoints (headcount, traffic, jobs, posts, reviews) need a Subscription or Enterprise plan.
- Call company.search first to get a UUID, or pass a website directly as company.
