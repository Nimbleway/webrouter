Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- status is deliverable, risky (accept-all, full mailbox or disposable), undeliverable or unknown; reason narrows it (accepted_email, low_deliverability, low_quality, invalid_email, invalid_domain, rejected_email, dns_error, unavailable_smtp, unsupported, timeout, unknown).
- Flag fields (acceptAll, disposable, free, role, disabled, fullMailbox) are the strings 'yes', 'no' or 'unknown', not booleans.
- A 402 means the account has run out of credits; a 503 can be retried.
- Addresses at sandbox.usebouncer.com (deliverable@, undeliverable@, accept-all@, disposable@, unknown@ ...) are free to verify while testing.
- Creating a batch, the synchronous batch queue and toxicity list jobs take a raw JSON array body and are not in this entry; the status and download calls read a batch created elsewhere.
