Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Results: ok (deliverable), ok_for_all (catch-all domain), unknown, antispam_system (blocked by anti-spam), email_disabled, dead_server, invalid_mx, invalid_syntax, smtp_protocol, disposable, spamtrap.
- Credits come in two pools: on-demand credits never expire, daily subscription credits reset each day while the plan is active.
- Rate limits are per endpoint and per second (10/s for the single checks, 5/s for contact finding); a 403 means the account is disabled.
- Email list upload, download, Deep Scan re-verification, deletion and inbox placement tests are not in this entry.
