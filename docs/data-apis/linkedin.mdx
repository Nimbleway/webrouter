## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `LINKEDIN_ACCESS_TOKEN` | required | A LinkedIn member access token from the OAuth 2.0 flow, with the scopes you need (w_member_social to post, openid and profile for identity); LinkedIn Developer Portal &gt; your app &gt; Auth |
| `LINKEDIN_API_VERSION` | optional | The Linkedin-Version header, YYYYMM. LinkedIn sunsets each monthly version after about a year (202511 on 2026-11-16). Default `202610`. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Only your own member data, or the company pages you administer: LinkedIn has no public search for people or posts. Permissions such as r_member_social are restricted to approved apps.
- Every call carries Linkedin-Version and X-Restli-Protocol-Version headers; URNs in paths and query values must be URL-encoded.
- Member access tokens expire; refresh them outside this catalog.
- Finder calls need the X-RestLi-Method: FINDER header and page with start and count.
