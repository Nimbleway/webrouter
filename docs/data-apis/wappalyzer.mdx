## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `WAPPALYZER_API_KEY` | required | A Wappalyzer API key (wappalyzer.com &gt; account &gt; API). Sent in the x-api-key header; the API needs a Business plan. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Every call spends credits; each response's wappalyzer-credits-spent and wappalyzer-credits-remaining headers give the charge and the balance. 403 also means out of credits.
- Rate limit: 10 requests per second; 429 when exceeded.
- Lead lists and suppression lists are not in this entry: they create and spend on account records.
