## Setup

|---|---|---|
| `ENGAGEMII_DATA_KEY` | required | an Engagemii data API key (starts with edk_) from the API access page of a free data account (https://engagemii.com/portal/data/api) |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Unit-based: every company record returned costs 1 unit; units are bought 5,000 for &#36;250 (5 cents a unit) and never expire; /info and /orders are free, and nothing is charged on an error. Free lookups are used first: a data account gets 20, one time; an agency account gets 1 as a sample, then 2 a month while the Agency plan is paid; a brand account gets none.
- 60 requests a minute per key across all endpoints. Every answer carries X-RateLimit-Remaining and X-RateLimit-Reset; past the limit the answer is 429 rate_limited with Retry-After.
- A record has the same columns as an Engagemii data file: the 0 to 10 AI visibility score with its on-page and off-page parts, AI engine reads, AI naming, the agent readiness and AI adoption indexes, category, location, tech stack and social profiles. Column meanings: https://engagemii.com/aeo/data/dictionary.

<Note>
- Starts a billed job when the website is not in the index yet: a paid lookup scans and scores it. If the scan finishes within about 20 seconds the record comes back in the same call; otherwise the answer is 202 scanning or queued with retryAfterSec, and the next call returns the record. A free lookup on such a website answers 404 not_found with reason not_indexed and starts nothing.
- Returns record (the full data-file columns), company (standard field names such as name, website, industry, companyType, country, city, phone, linkedin) and sources (where each value came from). A field not held is left out.
- A 202 filling answer means the record check is still running; call again after retryAfter seconds. completion.pending true means some cells finish in the background and a later call reads them. Every 202 costs nothing.
- Scans of websites not yet in the index are limited to 30 an hour and 100 a day per key (429 scan_limit_key); websites already in the index still answer.
- A second lookup of the same website within 24 hours of a charged one is free (allowance reread, unitsSpent 0).
</Note>

<Note>
- A read over POST: it only queries records.
- Metered: 1 unit per record returned. filters.publicCompanies 'only' (US public companies, SEC ticker and CIK on every row) costs 10 units a record and runs only with acceptUnitsPerRow 10.
- filters.scope defaults to 'us' (United States); send 'all' for worldwide.
- Results come best AI visibility score first. Paging: send offset 0, then the nextOffset of each answer until hasMore is false; never add the count to offset.
- A page with no record ready yet carries Retry-After (3, 5, then 8 seconds) and says how many records are still being completed. A filter or value that does not exist is refused with 400 before anything is read.
</Note>
