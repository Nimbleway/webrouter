## Notes

- Billed in credits (1 credit = EUR 0.01 list price). Failed calls cost nothing, except the +20 AI surcharge once an AI lookup ran; meta.request_credit_cost in each answer gives the real charge and meta.credits_remaining the balance. 402 means the balance is below the call's maximum cost.
- Rate limits per account per minute, all keys together, rejected calls included: fetch-organization 60, fetch-person 60, search and Signals share 60. 429 carries Retry-After.
- Profile and person calls can take up to 120 seconds (then a free 408), longer than call_vendor's fixed 60-second timeout; retry a timed-out call.
- Until the account's email is verified, every call answers 403. Monitoring, webhooks, token and key management are writes and are not in this entry.
