## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `GETPROSPECT_API_KEY` | required | A GetProspect API key (Settings &gt; API); it spends its workspace's credits. Sent in the x-api-key header. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Searching is free; emails spend email credits and verifications spend the USD wallet, only when something is found. 402 with X-Limit-Reached when the balance is out.
- Saving people or companies, lists, bulk jobs, exports, webhooks, phone reveals and the block list are not in this entry.
