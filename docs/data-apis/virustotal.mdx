## Setup

| Variable | | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| `VIRUSTOTAL_API_KEY` | required | A VirusTotal API key (sign in &gt; API key). Sent in the x-apikey header. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Public (free) API: 4 requests per minute and 500 per day, and it must not be used in commercial products or services or in business workflows that do not contribute new files. Premium keys lift both.
- Everything you submit or query joins the VirusTotal dataset, visible to its community and partners: never send sensitive or personal data (private URLs with tokens, internal hostnames).
