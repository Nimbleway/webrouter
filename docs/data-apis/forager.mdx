|---|---|---|
| `FORAGER_API_KEY` | required | A Forager API key (app.forager.ai). Sent in the X-API-KEY header. |

Put them in your environment or `.env`; [Data APIs](/vendors) covers the other ways.

## Notes

- Almost every path carries your numeric account id; get it once from forager.account.current.
- Searches and lookups are POST with a JSON body but only read. Each spends credits at the rate your plan sets for that kind of call.
- Filters that take lists of locations, industries, skills, keywords or organizations want Forager ids from the reference.* lookups.
- Job post search, bulk work email lookups, feedback submission and API key management are not in this entry.
